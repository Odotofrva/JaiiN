The story

A young artist learning one set at a time.

Nyla Jaii is the young creator behind Jaii Nails, a growing brand built from passion, creativity, and a love for nail design. From a very early age, Nyla showed a natural interest in doing nails, spending her time learning, practicing, and finding her own style through each set she created.

Daily, she takes time to study nail videos, practice new ideas, and learn from the work she sees in local nail shops. She is self-taught, patient with the process, and excited to keep improving with every set.

Her middle name is Jaii, so the brand carries something personal. This page is not just about booking nails. It is about supporting a young founder as she builds confidence, skill, and a dream from the ground up with love from her family behind her.

Jaii Nails Foundation

Jaii Nails Foundation is a future education program being created to help teen girls explore the art, discipline, and confidence of becoming a nail technician.

The foundation is inspired by Jaii’s own self-taught journey. After school, she watches tutorials, studies different techniques, practices what she learns, and visits local nail shops to better understand the craft. The goal is to one day give other girls the same encouragement, tools, and confidence to begin their own creative path.
