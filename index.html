Her story

A nail brand built from curiosity and practice.

Nyla Jaii is the young creator behind Jaii Nails. From an early age, she showed a natural interest in nail design—watching tutorials, practicing techniques, and paying close attention to the details that make a set feel special.

Her middle name is Jaii, making the brand personal from the start. This is more than a booking page: it documents a young founder developing artistry, customer care, discipline, and entrepreneurship with family support behind her.
